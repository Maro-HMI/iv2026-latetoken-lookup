Late Token Rules

+24 h per token For SO1–SO4 and A1, not A2. Every started 24 h costs one whole token, used automatically. If your tokens don't cover the whole delay, none are used.
Earn tokens You start with 1 token. Earn +0.5 for each Wooclap at the start of lectures L2 to L9 (up to +4).
Grade bonus Each whole token left at the end of the course adds +0.1 to your final grade. Half tokens don't count.
